How do we present reviews?

Source

Reviews marked as Google or Booking come from publicly available profiles on those services. The source is shown with every statement.

No independent verification

Adago Stay does not independently verify whether every reviewer completed a stay. We rely on information and labels provided by the source platform.

Selected feedback

The website presents selected statements, not the complete collection of all reviews. The section mainly shows positive guest experiences.

Shortening and paraphrasing

Longer reviews may be shortened or paraphrased for readability. Their meaning is preserved and the submitted rating is not increased.

Important: ratings and the number of reviews may change on the source platforms. Website information is updated periodically, so the current Google rating is best checked directly on Google.
