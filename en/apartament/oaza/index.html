Oaza apartment in Szczawno-Zdrój

A stylish apartment in Szczawno-Zdrój near Wałbrzych, ideal for up to 4 guests. Guests have access to a double bed, a sofa bed, TV with Netflix, fast Wi‑Fi, a fully equipped kitchenette with a fridge, kettle and coffee machine, and a bathroom with a shower. Oaza is a great choice for a romantic weekend, a spa-town stay or a family trip close to Książ Castle.
